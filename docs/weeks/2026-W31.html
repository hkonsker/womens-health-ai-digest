AI in Ob/Gyn and REI Time-Lapse Culture 7

Time-Lapse Culture Improves Development and Pregnancy Outcomes of Sibling Normally Fertilized Oocytes: A Prospective Multicenter Study.

Reprod Med Biol · Mio Y et al. · Jan 1, 2026

A prospective multicenter sibling-oocyte study randomizing 3244 mature oocytes found higher Day 2 good quality embryo rates and blastocyst rates with time-lapse culture, with pregnancy rate differences not reaching significance. The sibling-oocyte randomization controls for patient factors better than most time-lapse literature, though it tests the culture environment rather than algorithmic selection and is underpowered for live birth.

PMOS Linked To Increased Cardiovascular Disease Risk, Study Finds

FemTech Insider · Jul 30, 2026

A Lancet Obstetrics, Gynaecology and Women's Health analysis of health records from 413,450 women aged 18 to 50 found elevated atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease risk independent of conventional risk factors. AI in Ob/Gyn and REI Gestational Diabetes Screening 6

Can we optimize selective screening of gestational diabetes mellitus? A multivariable predictive model on population-based data from the French National Perinatal Surveys.

J Gynecol Obstet Hum Reprod · Badra L et al. · Jul 24, 2026

Using two population-based French National Perinatal Surveys with over 10,000 women each for derivation and external validation, a multivariable model for selective gestational diabetes screening improved specificity only from 0.46 to 0.49 at the same screening rate. The honest negative conclusion, that routine clinical predictors already capture nearly all available signal, is a useful check on enthusiasm for more complex GDM risk models.

AI in Ob/Gyn and REI PCOS Phenotyping 5

Data-driven endocrine-metabolic phenotypes in young women with polycystic ovary syndrome and associations with cardiometabolic risk markers.

J Endocrinol Invest · Piórkowska N et al. · Jul 28, 2026

PCA plus Gaussian mixture clustering in 1032 young women with PCOS produced two subgroups, with a small thyroid/autoimmune-enriched cluster showing a higher prevalence of elevated TG/HDL ratio. The finding is honest about being exploratory: the cluster structure was largely driven by thyroid variables, it is single-center and cross-sectional, and it does not yet change management.

AI in Ob/Gyn and REI Cerclage Outcomes 5

Maternal and obstetric factors associated with successful vaginal cerclage treatment: a prospective cohort study.

Eur J Obstet Gynecol Reprod Biol · Lamens JT et al. · Jul 23, 2026

In a prospective cohort of 222 patients with prior spontaneous preterm birth who received cerclage, maternal age, smoking, gestational age at placement, and cervical length predicted preterm birth before 32 weeks, stratifying risk from 7% to 80%. Prospective and clinically actionable for counselling, but the history-based group model was unreliable, the high-risk group had only 15 patients, and there is no external validation.

AI in Ob/Gyn and REI LLM Teleconsultation 5

Performance of Large Language Model-Based Chatbots in Primary Health Care Teleconsultations: A Comparison Between Human and Artificial Intelligence-Generated Responses.

Telemed J E Health · Barros GDM et al. · Jul 25, 2026

Blinded raters compared 180 human and LLM-generated responses to real primary care teleconsultation queries in cardiology, endocrinology, and OB-GYN, finding AI equal or better on quality criteria and better on comprehensibility. A real-world query set is a strength, but it is a retrospective rating study with no patient outcomes and raters could often identify authorship.

AI in Ob/Gyn and REI Sperm Detection 5

AI-based techniques in sperm detection.

Curr Opin Urol · Gemmell LC et al. · Jul 23, 2026

Narrative review summarizing deep learning, augmented reality, and microfluidic approaches to finding rare sperm in azoospermia and cryptozoospermia, with early reports of recovery in samples previously called sperm-negative. Directly on this reader's beat and a useful orientation, but it is a review with no new data and the authors note validation is still lacking.

Kompass Diagnostics Scales Rapid Finger-Prick Hormone Testing

FemTech Insider · Jul 29, 2026

Kompass licensed a University of Chicago silicon biosensor platform that returns estradiol and other hormone results from a finger prick in about ten minutes, with panels built for IVF and egg freezing monitoring. Point-of-care estradiol could change cycle monitoring logistics, but no accuracy data against standard immunoassay is presented here.
