Clinical AI Genotype Prediction Trial 9

AI-based clinician decision support system for diagnosis of inherited retinal diseases: a multicenter, randomized trial.

Nat Med · Jia H et al. · Jul 24, 2026

Retina4IRD, a vision transformer trained on multimodal imaging from 1,843 genetically confirmed patients across three countries, predicts 17 IRD genotype categories and was tested in a 295-patient randomized trial where AI-assisted specialists reached 88.5% top-5 genetic accuracy versus 67.3% for specialists alone. This is a rare combination of external validation and a randomized clinical endpoint with downstream management effects, a template for how diagnostic AI should be evaluated even outside ophthalmology.

Clinical AI Speech Biomarkers 7

A deep learning model for speech-based prediction of clinical scores in people with Huntington's disease: a longitudinal study with cross-sectional replication.

Lancet Digit Health · Le Moine Veillon C et al. · Jul 23, 2026

A deep learning model on speech recordings predicted UHDRS clinical scores in Huntington's disease across four prospective cohorts, with 10-fold cross-validation, external replication in 110 patients, and agreement comparable to clinician ratings plus correspondence with striatal atrophy on MRI. Useful as a template for validating passive digital biomarkers longitudinally, though cohorts are modest in size and the endpoint is a rating scale rather than a hard clinical outcome.

Femtech and Digital Health Fertility Algorithm Clearance 7

Natural Cycles Receives Sixth FDA Clearance for AI-Powered Fertility Algorithm

FemTech Insider · Jul 28, 2026

Natural Cycles secured its sixth FDA clearance, this time for a next-generation fertility algorithm combining statistical modeling with machine learning trained on tens of millions of real-world cycle data points. This is a regulatory milestone for a cleared algorithmic contraceptive tool that sits squarely in reproductive AI, though the announcement gives no performance data or comparison against the prior algorithm.

Clinical AI Psychosis Risk LLMs 6

Evaluating large language models for assessment of psychosis risk.

NPJ Digit Med · Zhu T et al. · Jul 23, 2026

Eleven open-weight LLMs were benchmarked on 678 clinical high risk for psychosis interview transcripts from 373 participants, with the best model reaching 0.80 accuracy, 0.93 sensitivity, 0.58 specificity, and symptom score ICCs around 0.75 against researcher ratings. Careful evaluation including confabulation rates and site variation, but it is retrospective transcript scoring in a heavily enriched sample, so specificity and real workflow value remain unproven.

AI in Ob/Gyn and REI Cervical Cytology Workflow 6

Improving laboratory workforce efficiency using AI-assisted digital cytology within an HPV-based cervical screening programme: A model-based evaluation for the NHS Cervical Screening Programmes.

BMJ Open · Wilson A et al. · Jul 28, 2026

A decision-tree model parameterised from published data and expert input estimated that AI-assisted digital cytology (Genius) would cut cervical cytology review from 12.9 to 4.0 minutes per slide across the NHS screening programme, a 69% productivity gain. Relevant to anyone thinking about deploying AI in cervical screening at national scale, but it is a workflow model rather than a prospective evaluation, and it reports no accuracy or clinical outcome data.

AI in Ob/Gyn and REI Cervical Screening AI 6

An explainable machine learning approach for cervical cancer screening: decoding morphological diagnostic drivers.

Acta Clin Belg · Chang H et al. · Jul 29, 2026

A retrospective audit of 5,000 archived liquid-based cytology slides in a Chinese network found the LBC-NET pipeline detected HSIL+ with 92.5% sensitivity versus 84.2% for a blinded senior cytopathologist panel, with stable performance in menopausal and infected subgroups and 63% faster review. The head-to-head comparison against expert readers and subgroup robustness are strengths, but it is retrospective, single-network, and lacks external validation or prospective deployment.

Clinical AI AI Communication Stress 5

Perceptions of simulated artificial intelligence in medical consultations: associations with stress, memory, and perceived credibility.

NPJ Digit Med · Mayer CJ et al. · Jul 29, 2026

In a Wizard of Oz experiment with 163 healthy volunteers receiving simulated bad news, purportedly AI consultations produced lower stress and cortisol than human clinicians, while the chatbot format yielded the worst memory retrieval. An unusual look at how patients respond to AI-delivered communication, though the simulated interaction, healthy participants, and fake AI limit clinical transfer.

AI in Ob/Gyn and REI Oocyte Scoring 5

AI-derived oocyte morphology and follicular fluid biomarkers in donors.

Reproduction · Herrero Y et al. · Jul 29, 2026

In 49 young oocyte donors, AI-derived oocyte morphology scores correlated with follicular fluid glucose, cholesterol, HDL, AMH, and HSPG2 but not with triglycerides, LDL, LH, or FSH. It offers a biological interpretation of what AI oocyte scores may capture, which is directly relevant to REI, but the sample is tiny, biomarkers were pooled per woman, and no link to live birth is shown.

AI in Ob/Gyn and REI PCOS Phenotyping 5

Data-driven endocrine-metabolic phenotypes in young women with polycystic ovary syndrome and associations with cardiometabolic risk markers.

J Endocrinol Invest · Piórkowska N et al. · Jul 28, 2026

PCA plus Gaussian mixture clustering in 1032 young women with PCOS produced two subgroups, with a small thyroid/autoimmune-enriched cluster showing modestly higher TG/HDL ratios. The cohort is a decent size and directly relevant to reproductive endocrinology, but the structure is cross-sectional, unvalidated externally, and the authors themselves call it exploratory.

AI in Ob/Gyn and REI LLM Teleconsultation 5

Performance of Large Language Model-Based Chatbots in Primary Health Care Teleconsultations: A Comparison Between Human and Artificial Intelligence-Generated Responses.

Telemed J E Health · Barros GDM et al. · Jul 25, 2026

Blinded raters compared 180 real teleconsultation answers from Brazilian primary care specialists, including obstetrics and gynecology, against LLM-generated responses and found AI equal or better on most quality criteria and superior on comprehensibility. It is a useful signal for LLM decision support in teleconsultation, but the design is a retrospective text comparison with no patient outcomes and raters could often identify authorship.
