AI in Ob/Gyn and REI PCOS Phenotyping 5

Data-driven endocrine-metabolic phenotypes in young women with polycystic ovary syndrome and associations with cardiometabolic risk markers.

J Endocrinol Invest · Piórkowska N et al. · Jul 28, 2026

PCA plus Gaussian mixture clustering in 1032 young women with PCOS found only two subgroups, dominated by a small thyroid/autoimmune-enriched cluster with slightly worse TG/HDL. Reasonable size and directly relevant to reproductive endocrinology, but cross-sectional, single-center, and the authors themselves call the latent structure exploratory and unvalidated.

AI in Ob/Gyn and REI LLM Teleconsultation 5

Performance of Large Language Model-Based Chatbots in Primary Health Care Teleconsultations: A Comparison Between Human and Artificial Intelligence-Generated Responses.

Telemed J E Health · Barros GDM et al. · Jul 25, 2026

Blinded raters compared 180 real teleconsultation answers from a Brazilian primary care telehealth service against LLM-generated responses across cardiology, endocrinology, and OB/GYN, finding AI equal or better on quality and comprehensibility with no difference in risk. A useful real-query benchmark, though it is a retrospective rating study with no patient outcomes and raters could often tell which answers were AI.

AI in Ob/Gyn and REI Sperm Detection AI 5

AI-based techniques in sperm detection.

Curr Opin Urol · Gemmell LC et al. · Jul 23, 2026

A focused review of AI-assisted sperm detection in azoospermia and cryptozoospermia, covering deep learning search tools, augmented reality aids for embryologists, and AI-microfluidic isolation, with early reports of sperm recovery in previously sperm-negative samples. Well aligned with the reader's field and a good orientation to a fast-moving niche, but it is a review with no new or externally validated data.
