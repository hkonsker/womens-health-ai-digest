AI in Ob/Gyn and REI PCOS Phenotyping 5

Data-driven endocrine-metabolic phenotypes in young women with polycystic ovary syndrome and associations with cardiometabolic risk markers.

J Endocrinol Invest · Piórkowska N et al. · Jul 28, 2026

PCA plus Gaussian mixture clustering in 1032 young women with PCOS produced two data-driven phenotypes, one small thyroid/autoimmune-enriched cluster with a higher TG/HDL ratio. The cohort is decent for a single tertiary center, but the clustering is cross-sectional, unstable in sensitivity analyses, and unvalidated externally, so it is hypothesis-generating only.

AI in Ob/Gyn and REI LLM Teleconsultation 5

Performance of Large Language Model-Based Chatbots in Primary Health Care Teleconsultations: A Comparison Between Human and Artificial Intelligence-Generated Responses.

Telemed J E Health · Barros GDM et al. · Jul 25, 2026

Blinded raters compared 180 real teleconsultation answers from a Brazilian primary care telehealth program against LLM-generated responses across cardiology, endocrinology, and OB-GYN, finding AI equal or better on quality and comprehensibility with no difference in risk. Useful evidence on LLMs in real referral traffic, though it is a cross-sectional rating exercise with no patient outcomes and evaluators could often identify authorship.

AI in Ob/Gyn and REI Sperm Detection 5

AI-based techniques in sperm detection.

Curr Opin Urol · Gemmell LC et al. · Jul 23, 2026

A focused review of AI-assisted sperm detection in azoospermia and cryptozoospermia, covering deep learning image analysis, augmented reality support for embryologists, and AI-integrated microfluidics, with early reports of sperm recovery in previously sperm-negative samples. Useful orientation for an REI-adjacent reader, but it is a review of small early studies rather than validated evidence.
